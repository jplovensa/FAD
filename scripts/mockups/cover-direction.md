# FAD cover direction

These are generated architectural concept visualisations, not completed-project photographs or construction drawings. The published programme briefs guide function and scale; roof geometry, landscaping, detailing, and materials are visual proposals subject to engineering and project review.

| Cover | Distinct visual identity | Programme basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R18 | Narrow studio, single-slope roof, coastal village | 3 × 6 m concept, studio with separate wet spaces |
| R36 | Modest gable roof, front garden, suburban street | 6 × 6 m concept, two bedrooms |
| R72 | Hip roof, shaded terrace, long side elevation, family garden | 6 × 12 m concept, three bedrooms |
| W | Industrial mono-pitch room wings, monitor-roof dining hall, gravel apron | Repeatable rooms, shared amenities, sanitation and laundry |
| E | Repeated small classroom gables, covered verandas, colourful learning courtyard | Teaching wings, supervised shared space, administration and sanitation |
| H | Flat parapet roofs, civic arrival canopy, blue-hour setting | Administration and staff/support buildings, accessible arrival and separate service access; clinical uses require specialist review |

Residential covers are in `public/images/residential/fad-r-{18,36,72}-render.webp`. Sector covers are `public/images/{workers,school,hospital}-concept.jpg`. The housing application card uses the R36 cover. The old shared residential banner is no longer shown, so the individual designs lead the section. Technical model studies remain separately authored. Use `render.mjs --studies-only` when updating them to preserve this cover selection.
